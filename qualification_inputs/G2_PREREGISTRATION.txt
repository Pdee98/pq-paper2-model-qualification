# Paper 2 Phase 4 verification

## Pre-registered G2 method (written before analytical execution)

The following rule is fixed before running Part C. No confidence level, seeds, horizon, selector or acceptance threshold will be adjusted after seeing agreement outcomes. A failed gate is reported for author review.

- Thirty independent replicates per configuration, with replicate-indexed SHA-256 seeds. Master identity: paper2-g2-v1. FIFO admission utilizations .3 and .6. Non-preemptive priority: block utilization .2, admission utilization .2 or .4. k=1; zero structural/state costs; no cache, pool or coalescing effects.
- One protected primary selector: ARM64 rep1, backend ml_dsa_44, operation verify, variant valid. E[S] and E[S^2] use precisely those selected raw rows, equally weighted; durations are seconds. No pooling with another host/session/backend.
- Fixed generation horizon T=26000/minimum positive class arrival rate, designed for at least 20,000 post-warm-up completed jobs per class. Stop arrivals at T and drain all jobs. Discard enqueue times before .1T. If a replicate fails the 20,000 requirement, report failure rather than extend/tune that replicate.
- Mean waits use jobs enqueued in [.1T,T]. Confidence intervals are Student-t, two-sided 99%, across the 30 replicate means (df=29; critical value 2.756385903670335). Each analytical value must lie inside its class/configuration interval.
- FIFO analytical wait: lambda E[S^2]/(2(1-rho)). Priority: W0=(lambda_block+lambda_admission)E[S^2]/2; W_block=W0/(1-rho_block); W_admission=W0/((1-rho_block)(1-rho_block-rho_admission)).
- Little's law per replicate/class: integrate queue-wait intervals clipped to [.1T,T] for mean number waiting; compare with observed arrival rate N/(.9T) times full mean wait of that arrival cohort. Require relative difference <=2%. Boundary carry-in/carry-out is reported through that difference, not removed by an algebraic identity. Every replicate/class value is reported.
- Part C stops if projected or actual execution exceeds 1,800 CPU seconds; wall time is also conservatively capped at 1,800 seconds. A6 and the unit suite are separate invocations. Their elapsed times are code-cost checks, not research results.
